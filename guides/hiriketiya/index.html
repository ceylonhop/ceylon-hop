Ceylon Hop guide · South coast

Hiriketiya

A small horseshoe bay just outside Dickwella on the south coast, ringed by coconut palms, with a gentle beach break on one side and a reef wave on the other. Brunch cafés, yoga in the jungle behind the beach, and a much slower pace than Mirissa.

Thinking about the train?

There’s no station in Hiriketiya. The nearest is Wewurukannala, on the line that runs on past Matara to Beliatta, about 10 minutes away; a few direct trains a day come from Colombo Fort, taking 3 h 40 to nearly 5 hours. Matara, about 20 km away, has many more. The coastal line was disrupted by a derailment in April 2026, so check before you rely on it. We drive it door to door, and the expressway is quicker.

Things to see & do

Five things worth your time

In the order we’d do them. Every one has hours, how far from town, how long to give it, and our honest tip.

1

Surfing the bay

One of the best places in Sri Lanka to learn. Facing the sea, the soft beach break on your right is where the schools take beginners; the fast left over the reef at the other end is for experienced surfers only. Board hire and lessons are all along the sand.

From townIn the bay
OpenDec–Mar best · mornings
Give it2 hours a session
EffortBeach break: beginner · reef: advanced
TicketBoard ≈ LKR 1,000–2,000/hr · lesson ≈ LKR 4,000–6,000
Good to knowThe reef end is sharp and full of sea urchins — wear reef booties, and stay on the beach break until you can turn. The line-up gets crowded from December to March, so paddle out at dawn.
2

The beach

A horseshoe of sand wrapped in coconut palms, small enough to walk end to end in ten minutes. Between surfs it’s for lying under a palm, a swim in the calmer corner, and a drink at the beach bars as the light goes.

From townIn the bay
Give itAs long as you like
EffortEasy
TicketFree
Good to knowIt’s a surf beach, not a lagoon. Swim in the calmer inside corner, away from the boards and the reef, and ask a surf school how the sea is that day.
3

Dickwella Beach

Hiriketiya’s bigger neighbour: a long, wide beach a few minutes away, with far fewer people — and, unlike the bay, where the jungle hides it, a clear view of the sunset. Good for a long walk when the bay feels busy.

From town12 min walk · 5 min by tuk-tuk
OpenThe last hour of light
Give it1–2 hours
TicketFree
From our driversGo for the sunset, then back to the bay for dinner — a tuk-tuk is about LKR 500. Agree the price first.
4

Blue Beach Island

A small island off Nilwella beach, 10–15 minutes east, that you reach across a sandbar at low tide — the walk out is half the fun. It’s free to visit by day. A local host has run overnight camping on the island, with a fire on the beach and dinner grilled for you.

From townNilwella · 10–15 min
OpenLow tide only
Give itHalf a day
EffortEasy · a short rock scramble
TicketFree by day
Good to knowCheck the tide times before you go, and wear shoes: the rocks are sharp and there are sea urchins. Camping is by arrangement and we couldn’t confirm this season’s details — ask us and we’ll check it’s running before you plan on it.
5

Dondra Head lighthouse

Sri Lanka’s tallest lighthouse — a 49 m white tower finished in 1890 — stands at Dondra Head, the island’s southernmost point. Walk round the base and out onto the rocks, with nothing but ocean to the south.

From townAbout 20 km · 25–35 min
Give it45 min
TicketFree to see from outside
Good to knowThere’s no ticket office. Climbing the tower is up to the keepers and often not allowed — plan to enjoy it from the ground.

Further afield

Worth a whole day, if you have one

15 mineach wayHummanaya blowhole, Kudawella

A blowhole in the rocks east of Dickwella that throws sea spray high into the air when the swell is up — best on a rough morning between May and September. Foreigners pay a small entry fee, about LKR 500.

About 1 heach wayMirissa

Whale boats from November to April, Coconut Tree Hill and a bigger beach scene, about an hour west. Easy as a day out, or as your next stop.

About 3 heach wayYala

The leopard park is about three hours east — better as your next stop than a day trip. Leave after breakfast and you’re there for an early start in the park the next morning.

When to come

The weather, month by month

Warm all year, 29–31° by day. The bay is sheltered and often has a wave out of season — what changes is the rain, the sea and the crowds.

Dec – MarPeak season

The driest, calmest months, with about 85–105 mm of rain, the best surf for beginners and the busiest bay. Rooms are limited — book early.

Best for surf
AprSeason’s end

Still warm and surfable, with showers building through the month and fewer people in the water.

May – SepSouth-west monsoon

Windier, rougher seas and showers, heaviest in May. The bay still often holds a wave, and it’s quieter and cheaper.

Oct – NovThe wettest months

About 260–280 mm of rain a month. Many places are quiet until the season picks up again in December.

Where to stay

Stay

Rooms in the bay are limited, and December to April books up early. Everything is a short walk from the beach.

Boutique

Salt House · La Playa · Jasper House

Salt House is a small boutique guesthouse about 60 m from the beach, with a plunge pool, a garden café and daily yoga; La Playa has three sea-facing rooms above the beach club; Jasper House is a surf and yoga retreat with a pool and a rooftop restaurant. Salt House and La Playa are tiny — book well ahead.

Your questions, answered

The things people ask us

Pick a topic, tap a question.

How many nights should I stay?

Two to four. Many people plan two and stay longer once they’ve settled in — it’s that kind of place.

Is it good for learning to surf?

Yes — one of the best places in Sri Lanka to start. Facing the sea, the beach break on the right-hand side of the bay is soft and sandy; leave the reef wave at the other end to experienced surfers. A lesson costs about LKR 4,000–6,000, and board hire about LKR 1,000–2,000 an hour. See the bay ↑

Is Hiriketiya good for families?

It suits couples, solo travellers and surfers best: the bay is small and social, and it’s a surf beach with a reef at one end. Children can swim in the calmer corner with you close by — ask a surf school how the sea is that day.

What else is there to do?

Yoga at Arise, a jungle studio about five minutes’ walk from the beach (about LKR 3,000 a class; book ahead out of season); skateboard lessons at the little skate park at Locos (about LKR 3,000); and a rice-and-curry cooking class at Rosie’s Kitchen.

Where do people go next?

East to Yala for a safari (about 3 h), back to the airport (about 3 h) or up to Ella (about 2 h 45). See the routes ↓

What’s the easiest way to get to Hiriketiya?

By car, door to door: about 3 hours from the airport, mostly on the expressway, or about 2 h 45 from Ella. We’ll stop wherever you like on the way.

Can I take the train?

There’s no station in the bay. The nearest is Wewurukannala, on the Matara–Beliatta line, about 10 minutes by tuk-tuk; a few direct trains a day run from Colombo Fort, taking 3 h 40 to nearly 5 hours. Matara, about 20 km away, has many more trains.

What about the bus?

Express buses run from Colombo to Matara; from there a local bus or a tuk-tuk takes you on to Dickwella and the bay. From Ella you’ll usually change at Wellawaya. It’s slow, and crowded in peak season.

What should I wear in town?

Swimwear on the beach; a shirt and shorts or a sarong when you walk up to the road or into Dickwella. Topless sunbathing isn’t allowed anywhere in Sri Lanka.

  • Bring some cash. There are ATMs on Hiriketiya Road, a short walk up from the beach, and in Dickwella town, 5 minutes by tuk-tuk — and not every café takes cards.
  • The bay is small and walkable. You only need a tuk-tuk or scooter for trips out to Dickwella, Dondra or Blue Beach.
  • Swim in the calmer corner, away from the surfers and the reef. Sea urchins live on the rocks.
  • Tuk-tuks aren’t metered. Agree the price first — about LKR 500 to Dickwella.
  • Poya (full-moon) days are dry: bars and shops stop selling alcohol.
  • December to April is busy with surfers and remote workers, and rooms in the bay are limited. Book ahead.
