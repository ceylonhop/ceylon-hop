Hi, we’re Roshen and Dasis — Co-founders of Ceylon Hop.

We’ve been friends for over 20 years, ever since middle school. Our shared love for travel has been the foundation of our friendship. I was the planner — the one who mapped out every detail — and Dasis was the life of the party, making sure every trip was filled with energy and laughter.

Over the years, we’ve organized countless trips for our friends, crafting experiences that were seamless, exciting, and unforgettable. Looking back, it feels like we were always meant to do this — building Ceylon Hop to help others explore Sri Lanka the way we love to: with adventure, joy, and ease.

Traveling around Sri Lanka without a car was always a challenge. Public transport can be confusing, and planning the perfect trip often felt overwhelming — there just weren’t enough reliable online resources to guide us. On top of that, most options lacked the flexibility to explore at your own pace.

We started Ceylon Hop because we’ve been there, and we wanted to create a solution that makes discovering Sri Lanka simple, flexible, and stress-free.

Why Ceylon Hop?

With Ceylon Hop, Dasis and I wanted to solve real travel problems — the ones we’d run into ourselves, and the ones we kept hearing about from friends we sent here. They all look something like this:

  • You’re having an amazing time in Weligama and want to stay a few more days to surf with the new group you bonded with. But your pre-booked tour is leaving today.
  • You spent $1,500 on a tour package that’s mostly great, but you’d rather skip a couple of stops and instead spend that time sipping Arrack Sours at Hiriketiya Beach.
  • You just conquered Sigiriya Rock Fortress and now want to take the most scenic train ride in Asia to Ella. You wish someone had figured out transportation to get you there and helped you book train tickets.
  • You’re road-tripping on a tuk-tuk or motorcycle, passing incredible culture, history, and natural beauty — but you wish you had a guide to color and enrich your experience.

We get it, and we solved it.

What you get with Ceylon Hop

We drive you between any two points in Sri Lanka — door to door, at a fixed price you see before you book. A private air-conditioned car takes up to 3 travellers with bags, a van up to 6, and the vehicle is yours for the journey. On popular routes you can also take a single seat on our shared service and pay a fraction of the private price.

✅ Flexibility — Change your plans or update your trip anytime. Just send us a WhatsApp message, and we’ll take care of the rest.

✅ Affordability — Why pay hundreds or even thousands of dollars for a rigid trip with hotels and add-ons you wouldn’t have chosen? With Ceylon Hop, you only pay for what you want.

✅ Authenticity — Ever taken a free walking tour in a random city and walked away thinking, “Wow! I learned so much about this place!”? That’s how we want you to feel about Sri Lanka with Ceylon Hop.

The service

Private transfers run every day, anywhere on the island. Our shared seats run on popular routes every Wednesday and Saturday. Between them you can reach all the places most people come here for — and we’re steadily adding experiences and curated tours in each of them.
