Sri Lanka is a small island that travels big. You can wake up on a beach, climb a rock fortress before lunch, and be watching the mist roll through tea country by evening. The distances look tiny on a map — and then you discover that 120 km takes three and a half hours on a road that winds through every village in between.

That gap between the map and the day is the thing we built Ceylon Hop to close. Here’s how travellers actually use us, and how to decide what suits your trip.

A private car or van, between any two points

The simplest way to travel here is to be driven. Tell us where you’re starting and where you’re going — any two points on the island, not a fixed list of stops — and you get a fixed price before you book. An air-conditioned car takes up to 3 travellers with bags; a van takes up to 6. The vehicle is yours for that journey: you leave when you want, and you stop where you like along the way.

That last part matters more than people expect. The drive from Colombo Airport to Ella passes waterfalls, a spice garden or two, and at least one roadside stall worth pulling over for. Tell your driver in the morning and it becomes part of the day rather than a detour you couldn’t ask for.

Or share a seat and pay a fraction

On popular routes we run a shared service every Wednesday and Saturday. You take a seat rather than the whole vehicle, you travel with a handful of other travellers, and you pay a fraction of the private price. It’s the right call if your dates are flexible and your budget matters more than your schedule.

If your route or your date doesn’t line up with the shared days, the private transfer is simply the answer — and the price you’re quoted is the price you pay.

Stringing several stops together

Most people don’t come to Sri Lanka for one journey. They come for a loop: airport, the cultural triangle, the hill country, the south coast, back to the airport. You can build that trip stop by stop and see what the whole thing costs before you commit, or start from one of our ready-made tours if you’d rather not plan from a blank page.

Either way it stays yours to change. Plans shift — you fall for a place and want another night, or a swell arrives and Weligama suddenly matters more than the schedule you wrote in January. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll rework it.

What you can count on

  • A fixed price, quoted up front. No meter, no surprise at the end, no change fees.
  • Vetted, insured drivers who know the roads and drive them every week.
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before you travel.
  • A real person on WhatsApp, seven days a week — before, during and after your trip.
