Colombo Airport (CMB) to Sigiriya / Dambulla — questions

How long does the Colombo Airport (CMB) to Sigiriya / Dambulla transfer take?

Plan for approx. 150 km · 3h 15m by road. Your driver takes the fastest safe route and can add stops along the way.

How much is a taxi from Colombo Airport (CMB) to Sigiriya / Dambulla?

A private car is from $67 and an air-conditioned van (up to 6 people) from $89, fixed and door to door — the price you see is the price you pay. A shared seat is from $27.49 per person.

How does a shared seat work?

Pick the date you want to travel. When 3 travellers are going on that date the van runs, and everyone pays $27.49 a seat. Your card is saved when you add your name and is only charged once the van is confirmed — if it never fills, you pay nothing.

Can we stop along the way?

Of course. A private transfer is door to door and yours for the trip — tell your driver where you'd like to stop for photos, lunch or a quick sight and they'll build it in.

How do I book the Colombo Airport (CMB) to Sigiriya / Dambulla transfer?

Get an instant fixed price and book online, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange it. You pay securely online to confirm your booking.
