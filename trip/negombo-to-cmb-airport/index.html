Negombo to Colombo Airport (CMB) — questions

How long does the Negombo to Colombo Airport (CMB) transfer take?

The drive is about 15 minutes on 7 km of road. Your driver takes the fastest safe route and can add stops along the way.

How much is a taxi from Negombo to Colombo Airport (CMB)?

A private car is from $29 and an air-conditioned van (up to 6 people) from $49.99, fixed and door to door — the price you see is the price you pay. A shared seat is from $19 per person.

Is there a cheaper shared option?

Yes — this route runs on our Airport–Cultural Triangle shared service (Wed & Sat). A single seat is from $19, ideal for solo travellers and couples happy to share.

Can we stop along the way?

Of course. A private transfer is door to door and yours for the trip — tell your driver where you'd like to stop for photos, lunch or a quick sight and they'll build it in.

How do I book the Negombo to Colombo Airport (CMB) transfer?

Get an instant fixed price and book online, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange it. You pay securely online to confirm your booking.
