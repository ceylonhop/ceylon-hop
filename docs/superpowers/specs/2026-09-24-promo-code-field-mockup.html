On a phone

The side summary becomes the sheet behind the bottom bar. The bar's Total copies the summary Total, so it shows $81 with no extra work. The promo row appears in the sheet when it's opened.

The chip and "Due now" are what the customer sees on the Payment step. The chip wraps onto two lines if a long code needs it. Codes are at most 32 characters.

Rare, but handled

The code passed the preview, but someone took its last use (or it expired) before this customer pressed Pay.

No payment is taken. There is no "Try again" button. It follows the same pattern as the local-road refusal, so the customer returns to the Payment step. There the field is open with the message and Due now shows the full $90, and they choose whether to pay it.
