The route

The road to the Lion Rock

Beach town to ancient citadel in a morning — lagoon and palms giving way to spice country, then the dry-zone plains of the Cultural Triangle.

Leaving Negombo, the road skirts the lagoon before turning inland through rubber and spice country around Kurunegala. By late morning the scenery flattens into tank-and-paddy land, and then Sigiriya's rock fortress appears above the treeline — visible a full twenty minutes before you arrive.

  • Dambulla's cave temples are 20 minutes from drop-off — an easy same-day add
  • Climb Sigiriya at 7am the next morning to beat the heat and the queues
  • Pidurangala rock, opposite Sigiriya, is the photographer's sunrise choice
How does a shared seat work?

Pick the date you want. When 3 travellers are going on that date the van runs, and everyone pays $27.49. Your card is saved when you add your name and is only charged once the van is confirmed — if it never fills, you pay nothing.

What if nobody else is on my date?

Start the list anyway — it goes up instantly and other travellers can join. Or pick a date that is already gathering: the page shows you which ones are closest to running.
