Little marks with a sense of journey

Twenty-four new icons in the site’s existing language — 24×24, single 2px stroke, round caps, one saffron waypoint dot each. The dot is the signature: it’s the route-doodle endpoint and the orange “C” shrunk to a pixel, and it’s what makes a Ceylon Hop icon recognisable at 16px.

Born from the doodleThe homepage route doodle — two dots, one dashed arc — is the DNA. Dashes mean “journey”, dots mean “place”.
One waypoint dotEvery icon carries exactly one filled dot. Saffron in brand contexts; it inherits currentColor when mono.
Geometry, softenedTrue circles and arcs, 2px stroke, round everything. No sharp corners anywhere — same as the ticket cards.
Reads at 16pxMax two ideas per icon. Anything that needs three strokes to explain becomes an illustration, not an icon.
