Same six rules, drawn by a person

The last set was geometrically perfect, which is exactly why it felt machine-made. This one keeps your palette and shapes but breaks the machine in four places: the disc is a wobbly blob rather than a circle, every line gets its own noise so nothing is quite straight, the cream is printed slightly off-register from the outline the way a hand-inked sticker misprints, and each badge sits at its own small tilt. Nothing is symmetrical, nothing repeats.

Same paths, same palette. The difference is entirely in the render: wobble, off-register colour, a blob disc, and a tilt. Every badge draws its own noise seed, so no two wobbles match.

Colour off-registerThe cream is displaced on its own seed, so it spills past the outline here and pulls short there.
No straight linesEvery edge takes a small displacement. Chunky, slightly uneven, a bit overshot at corners.
Everything tiltsEach badge leans a degree or three, its own way. Rows never line up perfectly.
