P1

Fees: inline toggle chips, tray retired

Today a fee is two clicks and a whole extra card band, and once on, it renders twice (orange pill + ticked checkbox). Proposed: the three fees are the chips — one click, price at the point of decision, one source of truth.

P2

Remove leg: undo toast, not a confirm

A fully-priced leg currently vanishes in one unconfirmed click with no way back — while deleting a quote, marking a no-show and an unusual premium all confirm. A confirm dialog would tax every intentional removal; an undo toast keeps the click cheap and makes the mistake free.

P3

Manual km: make the risky state look risky

A manual distance renders as a solid input — it looks more authoritative than the auto pill, but it’s the state where the price stops tracking the route. Every leg of every reopened quote comes back manual, so this is the stale-km underpricing shape. Proposed: manual wears amber and says so; “auto” becomes a real chip.
