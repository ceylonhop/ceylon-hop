Not a tour package. Not a haggle at the curb.

If you’ve travelled Sri Lanka before, you know the drill: confusing buses, taxi quotes that change with the weather, and tours that move at someone else’s pace.

Ceylon Hop is simpler. Name any two points on the island and you get a fixed price for a private AC car or van, door to door — before you commit to anything. Your schedule, your stops, your trip.
